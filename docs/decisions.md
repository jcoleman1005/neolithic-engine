# Neolithic Engine — Decisions

Decisions are settled; change one only on purpose, record why here, and update the table in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

| Area | Decision | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repo | New repo `neolithic-engine`, seeded from `artificial-life` at `ca3e97f` | `artificial-life` stays as it is; nothing is merged back |
| Platform | The simulation runs as the Node server in Termux on the S21; Chrome on the same phone is the viewer | Already proven by `artificial-life`; no background-tab throttling |
| Hosts | One `SimulationHost`, run by the Termux server on the phone or a Web Worker for desktop development | Same code in both |
| Engine budget | At most 30 µs per person per tick on the S21, 15 µs on desktop | `artificial-life` organisms measured 177 µs on the S21 |
| Determinism | Seeded random numbers and a fingerprint check stay | Free here, and it lets an agent prove a refactor changed nothing |
| Pace | 1 tick is about 1 day, 360 ticks a year, about 3 ticks a second | About 30 years per real hour; a speed dial for fast-forward |
| World | 256 x 256 cells that wrap at the edges | Fog of war lifts where anyone has walked |
| Population | Grows with no fixed cap; aim for 10,000 to 20,000 | Arrays double when full |
| Currency | One energy number per person that leaks every tick | Zero means death; surplus above a threshold becomes a child |
| Behavior | Three local rules: climb, copy, touch | Following, raiding and tribute are names for patterns, not code |
| Jobs | None; labels come from what someone has done recently | Skill grows with practice |
| Genes | Eight inherited traits: strength, bravery, greed, piety, charisma, curiosity, wisdom, cruelty | Blended from both parents, plus mutation; list settled in Phase 3 |
| Culture | Each habit inherited from one parent at random, plus drift and rare innovations; habits shift with experience | Replaces blending (halved differences each generation). Tunables in `config.ts` |
| Groups | Leader links only, no group table | A tribe is everyone under the same root leader, computed |
| Combat | Abstract clashes for groups, individual injury up close | |
| Resources | Food, seeds, brush and stone, carried one at a time; meat piles are food on the land | Like Age of Empires II villagers. Gold only if raids need loot |
| Religion | Builds cooperation, backs leaders, binds group identity | Works through shared ritual raising similarity |
| Viewer | Strictly watch-only: zoom, pan, tap to inspect, auto-camera | Stick figures up close; dots and colored regions zoomed out. No input reaches the simulation |
| Phone Host | Neolithic Engine takes over S21 exclusively | Stop `artificial-life` service while Neolithic runs to claim full CPU headroom |
| Art Style | WorldBox pixel art (procedural atlas) | Procedural sprite atlas in code, crisp pixels (smoothing off, integer zoom steps 1x, 2x, 3x...), 1 px/cell terrain offscreen cache with elevation shading and dirty redraws, small tree sprites on forest cells zoomed in, direct ImageData blit zoomed out, stick-figure sprites with deterministic ID offsets zoomed in, 30 FPS cap |

| Direction | A toy-physics antfarm: visible, dramatic consequences on the ground | The goal paragraph in the roadmap was reworded; history and the chronicle are by-products. Drama priority: land and weather, buildings and stockpiles, raids and politics |
| Authoring | Physics only, rule 4 kept | World drama is a field, a cost or a reward (weather field, fire fuel, soil), never a scripted group event |

| Phase order | 5 Settle and farm, 6 Weather and disaster, 6b Animals and hunting, 7 Raids and defense, 8 Leaders and city-states, 9 Religion (optional), 10 On the shelf | Physical world before social ladder |
| Weather legibility | Consequences drawn on the ground | Dry brown grass, flooded riverbanks, black scorch marks. No opaque overlay over people |
| Animals | Phase 6b: dumb herbivores (meat piles) and simple wolves (danger emitters) | Wander-and-flee only |
| Phase 2 gate | Ongoing audit, does not block | Ticked only when the S21 confirms |
| No player tools | The poke brush was approved and then dropped before it shipped | The engine runs as a completely autonomous, untouched terrarium. Keeps determinism pure, removes reverse input plumbing, keeps the focus on autonomous toy-physics emergence |

| 2026-11-05 | Carried water | Water becomes an ordinary hand-carried item (one thing at a time) poured into small stores at camps; thirsty people can find and drink from stores. | 2026-11-06 | Healing and fear (S3x) | Wounds heal slowly at any energy, faster when fed and sheltered. Repeated defeat drives the loser away: per-person fear stacks with losses in a region, lasts while the person stays there, raises what staying needs and pushes away from the place of defeat. Only losers are affected, and no momentum rule is added. Children lose fear faster near a parent, and a little faster near an allied adult. Recorded with its edge cases in `docs/SETTLEMENTS.md` |
| 2026-11-07 | Place cost replaces fear (S3x) | Supersedes the 2026-11-06 fear row. Fear pushed people and ignored wins, so most adults became afraid and good land was abandoned. People now remember the net energy that fights in their region cost them (losses add what was lost, wins subtract loot, the memory fades over about a year), and the stay check counts it as upkeep. No push: people leave because staying stops paying. Children learn their parents' view while near them |
| 2026-11-07 | Contest replaces the belligerence gate (S3x) | The gate (habit + cruelty > 180) stops under 3% of touches, and nothing in the decision to fight looked at food or odds. A fight is now a contest over the food both could eat. Each side weighs the stake against the exact odds and the real cost of losing and of a wound. Belligerence sets risk appetite, and the weaker side may yield, stepping off unhurt. Allies and the culture gate stay as they are |
| 2026-11-05 | Resource-Motivated Clashes & Lethal Casualties | Replace arbitrary friction with material stakes (hunger, carried food, food heaps, and cold shelter crowding). Inflict strength-scaled flat energy damage (`clashFlatDamage`) and lethal trauma collapse (`clashLethalInjury`); fallen combatants drop carried food to land and log fatal casualties in `clashLog`. Chronicle reports "N fell" when fatal and "driven off without fatalities" when non-lethal. Switch `resourceClashEnabled`. |
| 2026-11-05 | Combat Courage & Battlefield Looting (Primitives 76 & 78) | Victor in an unallied clash claims loser's carried item (food, seeds, brush, stone) up to body capacity; excess or mismatched item drops to tile as a ground heap. Outmatched fighters calculate odds (strength + nearby allies) and disengage via retreat step before combat if bravery is insufficient, saving life and gaining caution. Switches `clashLootEnabled`, `clashCourageEnabled`. |
| 2026-11-05 | Validation: Directional Effect Size over Arbitrary Targets (Rule 10) | Replace brittle arbitrary target percentages (e.g. "exactly 25% starvation") with directional hypotheses and macroscopic effect sizes across multiple seeds. Avoids Goodhart's law and tuning contortions; requires effects to be macroscopically distinguishable from baseline noise and visually confirmed (Rule 13). Missed expectations are logged as findings rather than forcing numbers. |
| 2026-11-05 | Golden Crops & Active Tending (Phase 5e, Primitive 5) | Active foraging that leaves >= 25% cell capacity raises cultivation `tilled` (primitive 5 active tending). Cultivated cells (`tilled > 0.25`) gain regrowth speed boost (`tendingRegrowBoost`), while untended cells decay back to wild grass/scrub. Worked land with food >= 70% capacity turns bright golden amber with fine grain texture; upon being harvested down, it reverts immediately to dark rich brown tilled soil. Switches `goldenCropsEnabled`, `tendingEnabled`. |
| 2026-11-05 | Phase 6a: Hunting & Fauna Dynamics | Grazing herds deposit prey food scent into food scent field (`herdScentEnabled`); hungry humans climb food scent gradients to hunt. Hunters butcher meat into hands (`huntCarryMeatEnabled`) with strength and stone weapon scaling (`huntScalingEnabled`). Wolves scavenge meat heaps (`wolfScavengeEnabled`), track prey scent (`wolfScentTrackEnabled`), and face human counter-attacks (`wolfDefenseEnabled`). Stabilizes 50-year predator-prey-human ecosystem balance. |

| 2026-11-12 | Watchability: five ideas recorded, #3 first | The user found the world dull to watch: people hug the water, some farm, some hunt nearby animals, little else. Cause: the riparian fertility lift, shore fish and thirst all reward the bank, and open land is about 99% full, so `worthStaying` passes and there is nothing to travel for; hunting is a roll against an adjacent animal (`life.ts`), so nobody chases; and the person frame carries no action, so eating, drinking, nursing, building and fleeing all look the same. Ideas, all keeping rule 4 (land, bodies, costs, rewards): (1) uneven land that ripens by place and season, with slower grass regrowth; (2) herds that migrate with the 6b-1 moisture, and hunts that are pursuits ending in meat carried home; (3) viewer: an activity byte per person drawn as poses or icons, and the existing auto camera fed small moments (kills, births, finished walls, fights); (4) materials (flint, clay, timber) that are scarce and far from water, then wells, so trips wear spoke paths; (5) floods (6b-3) pushing camps off the bank in wet years, and wildfire (6b-2, parked) with regrowth that draws herds. The user chose to plan #3 first: it changes no outcome and shows what already happens. 1, 2, 4 and 5 go to the Parking lot. |
